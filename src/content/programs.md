# Programs

The Madison Chinese Dance Academy offers a diverse range of cultural and educational programs that enrich communities.

## Dance Lessons

Dance lessons for Chinese dances, ballet, lyrical, jazz, and hip-hop with associated cultural backgrounds.

## Dance and Chinese Art or Folk Art Performances and Demonstrations

Performances and demonstrations for public and private events.

## Lunar New Year Celebration Program and Special Events

A full-spectrum program for schools, libraries, senior centers, private and public organizations, museums, and conferences. Offerings include lion dance, dragon dance, Chinese dance, Chinese music, yo-yo, calligraphy, lanterns, kites, arts and crafts, and show-and-tell activities.

## Chinese Dance and Chinese Ballet Artist-in-Residence

This program brings classical technique, expressive storytelling, and cultural heritage to a community through workshops, rehearsals, and performances. Blending elegant movement, breath-driven expression, and contemporary creativity, the residency uses an Embedded Teaching and Learning approach to offer participants a holistic body-mind-heart experience across the performing arts of the East and the West.

## Holistic Tai Chi Walk

Learn to live the time-tested traditional Chinese ways that have been validated by scientific research for a better quality of life. The goals are to maintain or improve physical and mental health, emotional and behavioral well-being, mobility and balance, memory and cognitive abilities, as well as strength and energy (Qi).

## Speaking Engagements and Cultural Quotient Enhancement

Learn about useful Chinese cultural wisdom, including Chinese ways of healthy living, self-cultivation, thinking, managing, investing, and strategizing, as well as differences between Chinese and Western cultures and philosophies.
